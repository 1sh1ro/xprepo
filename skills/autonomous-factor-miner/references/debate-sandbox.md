# Pre-return debate sandbox

Use this before formula design in `ideas` and `campaign` mode. Its output is a mechanism draft, never a chosen formula or performance claim.

## Information boundary

Permit source material, registry mechanism summaries, seed cards, raw field dictionaries, identity rules, availability metadata, venue constraints, and declared research objectives. Do not provide candidate returns, IC, Sharpe, drawdown, return-derived factor correlations, final-test output, or prior prompts that reveal them.

Semantic similarity to the registry is allowed. Formal field coverage and signal-correlation checks are separate and occur before returns as required by the campaign.

## Roles

| Role | Required output | Reject when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Explorer | Causal failure, two-link chain, prediction, falsifier | Formula search disguised as mechanism |
| Quality/Value Critic | Accounting identity, cash support, valuation anchor | Weak cash support or incompatible periods |
| Growth/Information-Diffusion Critic | Improvement, acceleration, event timing, delayed discovery | Price momentum relabelled as fundamentals |
| Data Auditor | event/published/available/signal/entry mapping | Field is unavailable, stale, identity-unsafe, or silently proxied |
| Novelty Librarian | Nearest accepted/rejected/redundant mechanism family | Renamed prior failure or structural duplicate |
| Research Chair | Selection/rejection/narrowing with decisive evidence | Vote, taste, or unobserved-return based |

Use Explorer, Data Auditor, and Novelty Librarian at minimum. Use the two economic critics for fundamental and event ideas. One model can serve several roles only with separate labelled responses.

## Debate packet

Store mechanism and source IDs; a `returns_visible: false` assertion; each role's conclusion and material objection; nearest prior families and mechanism-level similarity; required fields and availability contract; any `blocked_data` reason; the chair decision; selection rationale; and falsification condition.

An unresolved data or novelty objection is not a request to backtest. Mark it `blocked_data` or `blocked_prior_failure`.

## Feedback rule

After manifest freeze the sandbox closes. Validation may choose among frozen variants only. Final-test results close the campaign; they may inspire a later broad research theme but cannot revise the current seed, mechanism, or formula.
